“The longer I’ve worked in this industry, the more I’ve come to believe marketing isn’t really about marketing at all.”

The room grew quieter. “I think it’s about understanding people. How they see themselves. How they see their future.”

“Why certain ideas resonate while others don’t. Why two people can look at the exact same opportunity and make completely different decisions.”

“And after spending decades studying consumer behavior, I’ve become fascinated by a different question.”

Her eyes moved slowly across the ballroom. “Why do people make the life-changing decisions they make?”

The room was still now. “Why do they choose one path over another?”

“One career over another? One relationship over another?”

“One opportunity over another?”

“Why do some people stay? And why do others leave? What are they really searching for when they make those decisions?”

“Most importantly, what are they hoping to find? And what price are they willing to pay to get it?”
